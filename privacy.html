Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026 · Raise HQ · myraisehq.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Raise HQ (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use myraisehq.com and the Raise HQ application (the “Service”).

1. Who we are

Raise HQ is a software product for founders managing fundraising workflows. Contact: support@myraisehq.com

2. Information we collect

  • Account information — name, email, company name, and authentication details when you sign up.
  • Billing information — processed by Stripe. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Customer Data — investor contacts, notes, pipeline state, documents, and similar content you upload or generate in the Service.
  • Usage data — log data, device/browser type, pages viewed, and approximate location derived from IP address.
  • Communications — messages you send to support or via forms.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service;
  • To process subscriptions and send billing notices;
  • To respond to support requests;
  • To detect abuse, secure the Service, and comply with law;
  • With your consent, to send product updates (you can unsubscribe).

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information with:

  • Service providers who process data on our instructions (e.g. Stripe for payments, hosting providers, email delivery);
  • Legal authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety;
  • Successors in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, subject to this policy.

5. Customer Data (investors and fundraising content)

You control the investor and company data you enter. We process it only to provide the Service to you. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to upload contacts and for complying with applicable privacy and marketing laws.

6. Retention

We retain account and Customer Data while your account is active. After cancellation, you may export data; we then delete or anonymise it within a reasonable period unless law requires longer retention (e.g. billing records).

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect data in transit and at rest. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. International transfers

If you access the Service from outside the country where our systems are hosted, your information may be processed in other countries. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, or to object to certain processing. Contact support@myraisehq.com to exercise these rights. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

10. Cookies

We may use essential cookies to run the Service and, if enabled, analytics cookies to understand usage. You can control non-essential cookies via your browser settings.

11. Children

The Service is not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the revised version with an updated date. Material changes may be notified by email or in-product notice.

13. Contact

Privacy questions: support@myraisehq.com
