The investor journey

Five stages. One continuous process.

Structure the entire raise around the stages that actually move capital.

01

Discover

Find the investors who actually back companies like yours, and know who to hit first.

  • Investor CRM built for funds, partners, angels, syndicates and family offices — related properly, not orphan contacts
  • AI fit scoring (1–10) against your company and round, with written rationale and T1 / T2 / T3 priority
  • Warm intro mapping: who in your network can introduce you, and how strong the connection is

02

Engage

Open real conversations at volume without sounding automated.

  • AI-drafted outreach (email, LinkedIn, X) written from your own company context — not templates
  • Graduated trust model: calibration (every message reviewed) → exception digests → optional auto for lower tiers. Top-tier always reviewed
  • Reply classification and routing: interested / not now / referral / auto-reply / no — pipeline and tasks update automatically
03

Close

Move from interest to signed to wired without losing the thread.

  • Data room with per-investor access, revocation, and view tracking
  • SAFE and term-sheet documents, capital-call invoicing and pay links
  • Allocation dashboard: target vs soft-circled vs signed vs wired
04

Manage

Keep every investor informed, and keep the round’s true state visible.

  • Recurring investor updates from a single source of truth
  • Clear view of commitments, outstanding docs, and open threads
05

Re-engage

Turn a “not now” into a cheque on the next milestone.

  • Every “not now” captured with what would change their mind and a revisit date
  • Milestone triggers the follow-up automatically

How it works

From intake to live in three steps

Designed so a founder mid-raise can get productive without a long implementation project.

1

Intake & context

Company, round details, materials, and any existing investor list. The system uses this as the basis for scoring and drafting.

2

Calibration

Every outbound message is reviewed by you until the system earns trust. Approve, edit or skip from the inbox.

3

Live

Exception digests with stated send times. Optional auto for lower tiers. Top-tier investors stay under human review.
